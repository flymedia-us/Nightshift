# Nightshift distribution and Safari verification

- **Official public distribution is exclusively through the Mac App Store.** Keep the source public and source builds supported. Use the App Store archive/export/verification workflow for official updates; upload or submit only when the user authorizes those actions. Do not publish or offer official app/ZIP/DMG downloads through GitHub, the website, or Drop Box. Existing ad-hoc artifacts and their instructions below are historical private maintainer testing; create or hand off another private beta only on an explicit request, rather than automatically after routine changes. Do not delete historical artifacts or backups.

- **Current preference storage:** use Safari’s `browser.storage.local` and standard `storage.onChanged` events. Settings and manual exclusions are independent per Safari profile and persist through normal restarts/updates. Do not restore the removed native shared-settings bridge, native messaging permission, cross-profile polling, retry journal, or shared UserDefaults/file store. Historical cross-profile behavior documented below is superseded by the October 27 storage change. Preserve existing profile storage and leave old native files untouched for rollback.
