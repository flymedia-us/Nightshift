Nightshift 1.0.20 (35) — pre-launch verification

• Switch Always Light, Always Dark, and System from the Safari toolbar and settings page. Verify the popup and settings appearance follow the selected mode.
• Add and remove manual website exclusions. Quit and relaunch Safari, then confirm the mode and exclusions persist.
• Use two Safari profiles with different modes/exclusions. Preferences must stay independent, while open tabs in the same profile update immediately.
• Check the four settings sections: your manual exclusions, site-marker detections, the Nightshift list, and the Dark Reader list. Try a website override and restore automatic rules.
• Verify website photos/video keep their colors. Check existing and newly typed text in Google Docs/Sheets, and confirm text saved while Dark is active remains black when switched to Light.
• Open settings using the popup's filled cog, and check the displayed website domain.

Report macOS, Safari, and Nightshift versions, affected public URL, selected mode, and reproduction steps. Avoid private page contents in reports.
