Nightshift is a Safari Web Extension with no account, purchases, analytics, advertising, or network service.

To review:
1. Launch Nightshift.
2. Click “Open Safari Extension Settings.”
3. Enable Nightshift and grant access to websites when Safari asks.
4. Open an ordinary HTTP or HTTPS website.
5. Add Nightshift to Safari's toolbar if it is not already visible, then open its popup.
6. Verify that Always Dark applies the dark theme, Always Light removes it, and System follows the Mac's current appearance.
7. Turn off “Enable on this website” and verify that the current site and its embedded frames remain unchanged.
8. Click “Manage excluded websites…” to add or remove site exclusions.
9. Enable Nightshift separately in another Safari profile. Appearance and site settings are shared locally on this Mac, refresh when returning to a page or within 15 seconds while visible, and survive restarting Safari. There is no iCloud or cross-device preference sync.

Always Light means Nightshift is inactive; it does not force a website's own dark theme to become light. Safari internal pages and other protected browser pages are intentionally unavailable to extensions.
